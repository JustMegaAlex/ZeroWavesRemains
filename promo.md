
### First YT short
During playtesting some people had complaints about the upgrade system. 

Old upgrades in my game looked like this. From the beginning your tech hub has just a single option to upgrade your default weapon. To add a new weapon you have to kill a delivery drone. The drop from the drone has 3 choices. One of them might or might not be a new weapon. And only after you looted a weapon you can spend money to buy the new added gun.

Yeah, sometimes I overcomplicate things. So I decided to fix that and replaced the old system with good old tech tree. Now all the weapons are availible for purchase from the get go. And buying a new gun opens its upgrade branches. Classic.

As for poor delivery drones, I just told them to carry tons of loot now
