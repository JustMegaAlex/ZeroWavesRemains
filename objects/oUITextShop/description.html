What is this game?

ZWR is a classic twin stick shooter in its core. It will test your reaction and precision skills for sure but not only. Figure out which weapons work better against particular enemies. Develop your own play style while climbing the tech tree.

Is it hard?

Expect challenge! But there's also an option to lower the difficulty if you're more of a chill player.

But is there a tutorial?

Yes! The game explains how to play it pretty thoroughly. But you can always skip the tutorial at any moment.

Controls

WASD  - basic movement
RMB/Shift - boost movement (fast move forward only)
LMB - shoot (who would expect)
Space - trigger the next wave (after you delt with the previous one)
F - interact with the shop and upgrades
1-2-3  - choose a weapon (if you unlocked it in the shop)
E/Q - next/previous weapon
