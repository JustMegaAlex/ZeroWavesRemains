My new goals are:

- having at least one person asking "I want more of this!"

The deadline for evaluating the results is mid-Feb. Until that moment I will spend about two weeks on improving the core of the game and adding content. After that I'll focus on upgrading the appeal of the game as well as beautifying the itch page
